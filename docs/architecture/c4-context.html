Document Repo — System Context (L1)

Single-user app. The Owner photographs personal documents; the system OCRs and itemises them with Claude vision, surfaces upcoming reminders, and emails a daily digest.

Owner

Single allow-listed Google account. Sign-ins outside OWNER_EMAIL are rejected by the Auth.js signIn callback. Captures are usually iPhone photos of receipts, prescriptions, warranties, etc.

Document Repo

Single-user, persistent free-tier stack. The L2 view shows the two deployable containers (Vercel-hosted Next.js app and Cloud-Run-hosted FastAPI processor) and the protocols between them.

Claude vision

Each document goes through two Claude calls: (1) classify into one of 13 doc types, (2) re-call with that type's tool schema to extract typed fields and reminders. Prompt + tool definitions are cached.

Storage split

Originals live in R2 (cheap, large blobs). Structured extraction lives in Postgres. The web app never holds files in memory — it mints presigned PUT/GET URLs for the browser and the processor.

Daily digest

A Vercel cron job at 09:00 UTC pulls reminders due in the next 30 days and ships a Resend email to OWNER_EMAIL. Sent reminders are flipped to status='sent' in the same request.
